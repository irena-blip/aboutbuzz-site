Questions

Everything you need to know about collecting and using video reviews.

How many videos do I get at the beginning?

Begin with a free trial and up to four verified video reviews. Discover how authentic customer experiences strengthen trust and drive measurable results.

How fast can I start using the videos?

Very fast, from 14 days to 1 month, depending on the time reviewers need for the product test. Once reviews are collected and approved, you can use them immediately on your website and in marketing.

Can I use the videos in ads?

Video reviews can be used on your website, social media, and in paid ads (Meta, TikTok, Google) for videos where reviewers have granted advertising permission. All videos include a required aboutbuzz watermark. Videos approved for ads can be used for one year.

Is aboutbuzz compliant with the EU Omnibus Directive?

Yes. The directive requires online merchants to verify that reviews come from real consumers who actually used or bought the product, and to clearly disclose how reviews are verified. Every review receives a clear badge showing shoppers and regulators exactly how it was sourced.
