Questions

Everything you need to know about collecting and using video reviews.

Do video reviews really increase sales?

Yes. We have tested video reviews with existing users, and the results and feedback were very positive. Video reviews helped increase trust, engagement, and conversions.

Why video instead of text reviews?

Video feels more real. People trust real faces and voices more than written reviews.

Who are the video reviews from?

From real customers who actually use and test your product or service. No actors, no scripts, only trustworthy video reviews.

Can I request a specific type of video?

Partially. Reviews are authentic and unscripted, but you can suggest what customers should focus on when testing.

What about high-ticket or bulky items?

You do not need to give them away. For premium products, we arrange on-location testing where verified testers visit your showroom or location to experience the product in person. Get in touch.

How do I add video reviews to my website?

With the aboutbuzz plugin for WordPress or Shopify. Setup is quick and simple.

Do I need any technical knowledge?

No. Setup is simple and beginner-friendly.

Is your logo visible on the video reviews?

Yes. All videos are delivered with the aboutbuzz logo included.
