About us

Trust starts with a real face and a real voice

After ten years in digital marketing, our founder saw trust fading as online content grew louder. People don't want more ads. They want the truth.

We believe the answer is real human experience: not paid influencers, and not AI-generated scripts. Think of the warmth of a neighbour recommending a good book. That feeling was missing online, and it is why aboutbuzz exists.

Our goal is to make the world a bit friendlier and a more honest place.
